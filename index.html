CONDENSED RULES

A shortened digital reference based on the WMDG Constitution.

1. StartEach player chooses one neutral country as their capital. USA, Russia and China cannot be capitals.
2. ResourcesPlayers start with 100 reserve infantry and 10 gold. Your capital gives 2 gold per turn.
3. Gold1 gold buys 5 infantry. Defences cost 2. Small ships cost 1; large ships cost 2. Small cities cost 8; large cities cost 15.
4. ExpansionAttack one territory per turn, or end your turn without attacking. Bordering countries can be attacked directly.
5. ShipsShips let you reach non-bordering countries. The original Constitution states a 4,000-mile maximum.
6. CombatCombat is resolved in 10-infantry steps using a coin flip. Heads removes 10 from the defender; tails removes 10 from the attacker.
7. DefencesUp to 3 per country. A defence is tested before normal combat: heads destroys it; tails stops the attack.
8. Player warsPlayer-versus-player attacks begin in round 10. A fallen capital eliminates that player and their remaining territories become neutral.
9. CitiesSmall cities generate 1 gold per turn; large cities generate 2. Cities remain when territory changes hands, subject to the Constitution's limits.
10. DiplomacyPeace treaties can be proposed at any point. They can block direct attacks, or direct attacks plus support attacks. Either player can give one-turn notice before ending a treaty.
11. WinThe last player remaining wins. Deals, peace treaties, sales and loans are part of the wider Constitution.
